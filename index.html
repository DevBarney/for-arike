Written for you, by Barnabas

Two names.One of them is mine.

Ayanbukola Deborah is the name the world is lucky enough to say. Arike is the one I keep. Soft in the mouth. Certain. Only ever for you.

The private name

Only I call you Arike.

Not a nickname the room shares. A name with one voice on it. If anyone else tries it, it will not fit the way it fits when I say it.

Both hands on the joy. No apology for it. That is the whole lesson of you.

A note

My girl,

I did not want a forwarded wish or a story that disappears by morning. I wanted a small place with your face in it, that you can open again when the day gets loud.

Eighteen looks like this on you: light on your shoulders, confetti that refuses to land, a smile you do not ration. Every year before this one was practice for the girl holding the room right now.

Stay soft where it matters. Stay loud when the room needs your laugh. Stay the girl I get to call Arike — not because the name is secret, but because it is specific. It is how I love you out loud.

Happy birthday, Ayanbukola Deborah. Happy birthday, Arike.

Barnabas
